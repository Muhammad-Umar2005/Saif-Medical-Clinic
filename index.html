Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions from our patients

What are your clinic timings?

New Karachi: Monday to Thursday and Saturday, 10:00 AM to 12:00 PM and 6:00 PM to 8:30 PM. North Nazimabad: Monday to Thursday and Saturday, 9:00 PM to 10:30 PM. Both branches are closed on Friday and Sunday.

Do I need to contact you before visiting?

Yes, please contact us before visiting the clinic. Call or message us on WhatsApp at 0313 8394708 to confirm availability.

Where are your branches?

Our first branch is in Sector 5G, New Karachi, near Muhammadi Masjid. Our second branch is in Sector 15B, Buffer Zone, North Nazimabad, near Aga Khan Laboratory. Maps are in the Contact section.

Is physiotherapy available?

Yes. Dr. Maria Zahid offers physiotherapy, Reiki, acupuncture and needling at both branches.

Can I get SUAMZ Pain Relief Oil delivered?

Yes, home delivery is available. Message us on WhatsApp at 0313 8394708 to place an order.
